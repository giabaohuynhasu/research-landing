Sample paper title: Example Research Paper

Abstract: This is a short sample used to demonstrate the Third Order Audit tooling. It contains one central claim and an explicit falsification condition: "If dataset X shows no effect in three independent replications (Smith et al. 2020; Lee et al. 2021; Kumar et al. 2022), then the main effect is false." The revision history notes that the authors narrowed the claim after a preprint critique in 2023.
