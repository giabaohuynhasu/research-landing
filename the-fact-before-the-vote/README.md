## Overview

The Fact Before the Vote is a four-volume study of what follows once the premise that judges and claimants must share biological origin becomes visible enough to need stating for the first time. The series asks what happens to legal precedent, to political authority, to public feeling, and to institutional practice, once recognition stops being the only thing standing between a candidate and consequence.
