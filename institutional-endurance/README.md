# Institutional Endurance — Volume III Quantitative Module

## Overview
Quantitative duration comparison module for *Institutional Endurance: Site, Template, and the Structures That Outlast Their Builders*, Volume III (*What Hasn't Ended Yet*).

## Methodology
Right-censoring-aware duration comparison on $N=10$ governing arrangements (8 concluded, 2 right-censored).

## Result
* Concluded median duration: 52.0 years (range: 1.6 to 259.0 years).
* Right-censored active cases: Bosnia/OHR (31.0+ years), Kosovo/UNMIK (27.0+ years).
* Both censored arrangements have outlasted short transitional administrations (Cambodia, Timor, Japan), but remain shorter than five of six preserved-authority regimes.
